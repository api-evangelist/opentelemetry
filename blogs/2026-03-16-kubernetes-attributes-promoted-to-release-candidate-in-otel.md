Over the past few months, the K8s (Kubernetes) Semantic Conventions SIG focused on stabilizing Kubernetes attributes used by OpenTelemetry Collector processors such as k8sattributes and resourcedetection . That work has now paid off: Kubernetes attributes have been promoted to release candidate status. Users can try this new schema via feature gates and provide feedback before the final stable release.
