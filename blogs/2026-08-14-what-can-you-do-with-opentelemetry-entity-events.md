Metrics, logs, and traces tell you how your systems behave . They are much quieter about what actually exists : which hosts, interfaces, switches, services, and volumes are out there right now, and, crucially, how that picture changed over the last hour, day, or quarter. That living inventory has stayed a blind spot in the open observability stack.
