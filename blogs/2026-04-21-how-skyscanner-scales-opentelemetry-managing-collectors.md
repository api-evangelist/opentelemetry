The Developer Experience SIG is publishing a series of blog posts featuring real-world OpenTelemetry deployments from companies across different industries and scales. This post features Skyscanner , a global travel search platform based in Edinburgh, Scotland. With 1,400 employees worldwide running over 1,000 microservices across 24 production Kubernetes clusters, Skyscanner’s journey with OpenTelemetry offers valuable lessons for organizations operating at scale.
